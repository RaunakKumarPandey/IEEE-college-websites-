    • ANALYTIQ Event

    It is an "Analytical & Logical Challenge." This means it focuses on problem-solving, math, and logical thinking.
    (August 28th and 29th.)

    Event Details

    It is being organized by the IEEE Student Branch of MMMUT Gorakhpur.There are two main rounds:

    • Round 1:Tick the Bit
      Type: Online. This round is done on a computer, like an online test.
      What it involves: Answering multiple-choice questions (A, B, C, D on a screen) about coding, logic, and possibly pseudocode and general programming concepts (e.g., in C++ or Java).

    • Round 2: Guess the Logic
      Type: Offline. This round is done in person.
      What it involves: Team-based in-person puzzle solving. People are shown working together at a physical puzzle table, where they have to explain the logic behind various brain-teasers and puzzles, similar to the dice and gear puzzles shown in the main graphic.

    Venue: MMMUT Gorakhpur Subramanyam Hall.

    Date: August 28th and 29th.

    What can I win?

  • The total prize pool for the event is ₹6,000 (Indian Rupees).
  • 1st Prize: ₹3,000
  • 2nd Prize: ₹2,000
  • 3rd Prize: ₹1,000
  • All winners also receive a Certificate of Excellence.

    • ClashOfMind(A Group Decision-Making Challenge). Event

    A competition designed for teams to test their ability to analyze situations, make decisions quickly, and work effectively together.
    (August 28th and 29th.)

    Event Details

    It is being organized by the IEEE Student Branch of MMMUT Gorakhpur To help students develop teamwork, communication, logic, leadership, and decision-making skills.

    • Round 1:The Brainstorm Arena (Preliminary Round)
      Type: can be Online or offline. This round is to test teamwork, communication, and logical thinking.
      Execution: Your team will get real-life problem scenarios to solve. You have to discuss and submit your final decision.

    • Round 2: The Decision Showdown (Final Round)
      Type: Live interactive round held in a seminar hall. This round is to test quick thinking, leadership, and decision-making under pressure.
      Execution: Finalist teams will face rapid-fire problem scenarios. You have to analyze quickly, make a decision, and then present and justify it to the judges.

    Venue: MMMUT Gorakhpur Subramanyam Hall.

    Date: August 28th and 29th.
